# 📊 TELECOM CUSTOMER CHURN ANALYSIS

A Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer churn, identify high-risk customer segments, understand the factors driving customer attrition, and evaluate the potential revenue impact of churn.

## 🔎 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the major challenges faced by telecommunications companies. Losing customers directly impacts recurring revenue and increases the cost of acquiring new customers.

This project uses customer-level telecom data to build an interactive **Power BI Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard** that transforms raw customer data into actionable business insights.

The dashboard allows users to explore:

* Overall customer and churn performance
* Churn patterns across customer segments
* Customer demographics and service characteristics
* Contract and payment behavior
* Revenue at risk due to churn
* Potential impact of reducing churn

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze the overall customer churn rate
* Identify customer segments with higher churn
* Understand the key factors associated with customer churn
* Analyze customer demographics and service usage
* Evaluate revenue exposure from churned customers
* Identify areas where customer retention strategies can have the greatest impact
* Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview

Provides an executive-level summary of the company's customer base and churn performance.

Key areas include:

* Total customers
* Churned customers
* Churn rate
* Revenue at risk
* Overall churn trends
* High-level customer segmentation

### 2. Churn Analysis

Explores the factors associated with customer churn.

The analysis can be used to understand churn across dimensions such as:

* Contract type
* Payment method
* Internet service
* Tenure
* Customer demographics
* Service subscriptions

This page helps identify patterns and segments that require greater retention attention.

### 3. Customer Profile

Provides a deeper look into the characteristics of the customer base.

The page focuses on understanding customers based on factors such as:

* Demographics
* Tenure
* Contract type
* Services
* Billing and payment behavior
* Customer segmentation

### 4. Revenue & Risk

Focuses on the financial impact of customer churn.

This page provides a way to explore how reducing churn could potentially affect the business.

It includes analysis around:

* Current churned customers
* Potentially retained customers
* Revenue recovery
* Churn reduction scenarios
* Financial risk associated with customer attrition
